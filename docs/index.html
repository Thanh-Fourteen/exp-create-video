DTG Video Assistant

A personal tool for drafting short vertical videos about AI.

DTG Video Assistant is a single-user application. It generates short vertical videos on AI-related topics in Vietnamese and uploads them to its owner's own TikTok account as drafts. The owner then reviews each draft inside the TikTok app and decides whether to publish it.

How it works

  • The app writes a script, generates narration and images, and assembles a 1080×1920 video — all on the owner's own computer.
  • It uploads the finished file to the owner's TikTok drafts through the official Content Posting API (video.upload scope, draft/inbox flow).
  • Nothing is published automatically. Publishing is always a manual step taken by the owner in the TikTok app.

Scope of access

The app requests only user.info.basic and video.upload, and only for the owner's own account. It does not read other users' data, does not post publicly, and does not share data with any third party. Details are in the privacy policy below.
