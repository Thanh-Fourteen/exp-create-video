Privacy Policy — DTG Video Assistant

Last updated: 4 August 2026

DTG Video Assistant ("the app") is a personal, single-user tool that creates short vertical videos and uploads them to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts. It is not offered as a service to other people, and it has no other users.

Who operates this app

This app is operated by an individual developer for personal use. Contact: teamtriscec@gmail.com.

What data the app accesses

When the owner authorizes the app with TikTok, the app requests these scopes:

  • user.info.basic — the owner's own basic profile info (open id, avatar, display name), used only to confirm which account the app is connected to.
  • video.upload — permission to upload video files to the owner's own TikTok drafts.

The app does not read other users' data, does not access followers, comments, messages, or analytics, and does not post publicly. Uploaded videos go to the account's draft inbox, where the owner reviews and publishes them manually inside the TikTok app.

What data is stored, and where

  • Access and refresh tokens — stored in a local file on the owner's own computer, readable only by the owner's operating system account. They are never transmitted to any third party.
  • Generated video files — stored locally on the same computer.

There is no server, no database, and no cloud storage. No data is collected from anyone other than the owner.

What data is shared

None. The app sends video files and access tokens only to TikTok's official API endpoints, for the sole purpose of uploading the owner's own videos to the owner's own drafts. No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any other party.

Retention and deletion

Tokens and video files stay on the owner's computer until deleted. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from TikTok's account settings, and can delete the stored tokens by removing the local configuration file.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not available to anyone other than its owner.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "last updated" date.
