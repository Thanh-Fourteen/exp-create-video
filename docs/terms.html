3. How it works with TikTok

The app uses TikTok's official Content Posting API with the upload (draft) flow. Videos are placed in the owner's TikTok draft inbox. The owner then reviews each video and decides whether to publish it, from inside the TikTok app. The app does not publish content directly and does not post on anyone else's behalf.
